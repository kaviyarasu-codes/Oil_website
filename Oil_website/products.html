Rich in monounsaturated fats and vitamin E, our groundnut oil supports heart health and provides antioxidant benefits. Its neutral flavor makes it versatile for both cooking and salad dressings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Wondering how long our oils stay fresh?

Our oils have varying shelf lives: Groundnut oil - 18 months, Coconut oil - 24 months, Gingelly oil - 12 months. All dates are from the manufacturing date and assume proper storage in a cool, dark place.

Are your oils organic?

While we source our raw materials from farmers who practice sustainable agriculture, we are currently in the process of obtaining organic certification. Our oils contain no additives, preservatives, or chemicals.

Is bulk buying available for your products?

Yes, we provide bulk purchasing options for restaurants, retailers, and wholesale partners. For special pricing and minimum order details, feel free to contact us directly.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied with our products, you can return them for a full refund or exchange. The product must be returned in its original packaging with at least 80% of the contents remaining.
